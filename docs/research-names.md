## Open questions

1. 四天王: keep "Shitenno" (matches title) or translate "the Four Kings"/"Four Heavenly Kings" in dialogue?
2. 番長 / 番格 / 頭: keep "bancho" or use "boss"/"top dog"? Need one rule since all three appear.
3. Chiaki: does the user want her surname anywhere (it never appears in the script)? Same for Katsuji, Yoneji, Hamada etc. — propose using only what the script uses.
4. 用高 = "Yoko" (correct Hepburn, looks female in English). Accept, or note it in the README?
5. School suffixes: "Teiken High" vs "Teiken High School"; "Rakusui Academy"; "Sasazaki North" vs "Sasazaki Kita".
6. Puns to adapt: たま袋 (Shimabukuro), あかみ/マグロ (Akane), 鶴だの亀だの (Tsuruta/Kameoka), Taison's tongue-tied ちゃれらー.
7. Unresolved names: スミスさん, めぐちゃん/むつみちゃん, ジョー (likely parody/background refs), 米中 expansion, 三迫 reading (みさこ inferred).
8. 井岡: teacher (per manga) assumed; confirm in-game sprite at 5-057.
9. 殿: "Tono" vs "my lord" vs "boss".
